# AI Tells — Complete Detection Checklist

Reference file for `humanize-writing` skill (Pass 3 + Pass 4).
Sources: Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing · jpeggdev/humanize-writing · blader/humanizer

---

## TIER 1 — Extreme Red Flags (3+ = almost certainly AI)

### Verbs
delve / delve into, leverage (as verb), unpack, dive deep / dive into, navigate (abstract), explore ("explore the possibilities"), foster, facilitate, empower, streamline, harness ("harness the power of"), underscore ("this underscores the importance")

### Nouns
landscape ("the AI landscape"), ecosystem (metaphorical), tapestry ("rich tapestry of"), realm ("in the realm of"), paradigm, synergy / synergies, nuance (overused)

### Adjectives
robust, comprehensive, holistic, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, groundbreaking, transformative, game-changing, innovative (generic), dynamic (generic), vibrant (generic), pivotal

### Phrases
"it's worth noting that", "it's important to note that", "it's important to understand", "one must consider", "at the end of the day", "moving forward", "in today's world", "in today's fast-paced world", "in the digital age", "the world of [X]", "as we move into", "in the ever-evolving", "in an increasingly [adj] world"

## TIER 2 — Strong Signals

### Transition Words (overused)
Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally, In addition, Subsequently, Nevertheless, Nonetheless, Therefore (overused), Thus (overused), Hence (overused), Consequently, In conclusion, To summarize, In summary, Lastly, Firstly / Secondly / Thirdly (formulaic)

### Filler Hedges
"Generally speaking", "In most cases", "Typically speaking", "It's generally accepted that", "Many would argue that", "Some might say", "One could argue", "It can be said that", "There is no denying that"

### Vague Attribution
"Studies show that...", "Research suggests that...", "Experts agree that...", "According to experts...", "Scientists have found that...", "Evidence indicates that..."

### Sycophantic Openers
"Great question!", "That's a fascinating topic!", "Absolutely!", "Certainly!", "Excellent question!", "That's an interesting point!"

### Chatbot Artifacts
"As an AI language model...", "I'd be happy to help...", "Feel free to ask if you have...", "I hope this helps!", "Let me know if you need...", "Is there anything else you'd like to know?"

## TIER 3 — Contextual Signals

### Significance Inflation
pivotal moment / role, watershed moment, critical juncture, landmark decision, nestled in / nestled among, situated amidst, bustling (generic), thriving (generic), flourishing (generic)

### Promotional Voice
"world-class", "best-in-class", "industry-leading", "unparalleled", "unrivaled", "second to none", "top-tier"

### Hollow Affirmations
"Clearly", "Obviously", "Naturally" (discourse marker), "Of course" (within text), "Without a doubt", "Undeniably"

## GRAMMAR-LEVEL PATTERNS (Pass 4)

### Copula Avoidance
| AI Version | Human Version |
|-----------|--------------|
| "This represents a challenge" | "This is a challenge" |
| "This serves as a foundation" | "This is a foundation" |
| "This constitutes a problem" | "This is a problem" |
| "This functions as a bridge" | "This bridges X and Y" |
| "This acts as a catalyst" | "This triggers X" |

### Opening -ing Phrases (red flag)
"Recognizing the importance of...", "Building on this foundation...", "Understanding the context...", "Drawing from extensive research...", "Considering the implications..."

### Synonym Cycling
Look for: use / utilize / employ / leverage / harness rotating in the same text.

### False Precision Ranges
"between three and five" → concrete number; "in a matter of days or weeks" → be specific

### Passive Voice Overuse
"It can be seen that..." → "You see this in..."
"It should be noted that..." → just say the thing
"This has been demonstrated by..." → name the actor

## STRUCTURAL PATTERNS (Pass 1)

- Intro paragraph + exactly 3 bullet points + summary = AI signature
- Every section same number of paragraphs
- Every bullet point same length (1-2 sentences)
- "Key Takeaways:" or "In summary:" section at end
- Numbered lists where order doesn't matter
- Exactly 3 or 5 items in every list

---

## RHYTHM PATTERNS (Pass 5)

- Sentence length SD < 8 words = suspicious (metronomic)
- Em dash (—) in every paragraph
- Bold in every section (not just key terms)
- Bullet points for everything including 2-item lists
- Headers for very short sections
